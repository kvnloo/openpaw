# Behavioral rehabilitation of extremely fearful dogs: Report on the efficacy of a treatment protocol

Collins K, Miller K, Zverina L, Patterson-Kane E, Cussen V, Reid P (ASPCA). Applied Animal Behaviour Science 254 (2022) 105689.

[Source](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.applanim.2022.105689)

Numbers above were read from the full text (2026-11-27): desensitization and counterconditioning plus operant protocols for socialization with people, leash application and walking, and handling; one 15-minute session a day, five days a week; helper dogs in sessions until the final stages. Adopter survey: 137 of 297 adopters replied (88% very, 8% somewhat satisfied). Used by Learn card `c-toss-away`.
