main_finding: "The first time a muzzle was worn, 65.3% of owners saw the dog try to paw it off. Habituation without food went with dogs staying calm less often and more pawing; several weeks of training with treats went with less pawing and less avoidance when fastening, and using food with more dogs putting their own snout in. 161 owners (12.9%) reported physical effects (fur or skin damage, thermoregulation, eyes, gut); 17.2% chose a fit too close to the nose and 14.2% one that did not let the mouth open."
