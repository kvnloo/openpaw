# Fear-related behavior and aggression

## What is supported
Large caregiver surveys reported co-occurrence between fear and aggression, and dog or household characteristics were also associated with behavior reports. [Salonen 2020](../cards/salonen2020-prevalence-comorbidity-and-breed-differences-in-canine-anxiety.md) [Mikkola 2021](../cards/mikkola2021-aggressive-behaviour-is-affected-by-demographic-environmental-and.md)

OpenPaw should record who or what the behavior was directed toward, the observable action, and who reported it, rather than label the dog by a presumed motivation. [Pellowe 2025](../cards/pellowe2025-dog-guardian-interpretation-of-familiar-dog-aggression-questions.md) [Pedretti 2023](../cards/pedretti2023-appeasement-function-of-displacement-behaviours-dogs-behavioural-displays.md) [Shnookal 2025](../cards/shnookal2025-exploring-trainer-insights-and-understanding-of-on-lead.md)

## What is contested
Breeds differed in some surveys, but inconsistent aggression definitions and uneven representation limit breed-level comparisons, and one laboratory study found few breed differences overall. [Salonen 2020](../cards/salonen2020-prevalence-comorbidity-and-breed-differences-in-canine-anxiety.md) [Sartore 2025](../cards/sartore2025-genetic-and-gene-by-environment-influences-on-aggressiveness.md) [Seymour 2026](../cards/seymour2026-investigation-of-breed-related-variation-in-judgement-bias.md)

## What is unknown
These studies do not establish individual escalation probabilities, or that historical adversity or a candidate biomarker identifies why a given encounter occurred. [Espinosa 2025](../cards/espinosa2025-influence-of-early-life-adversity-and-breed-on.md) [Riggio 2020](../cards/riggio2020-serotonin-and-tryptophan-serum-concentrations-in-shelter-dogs.md) [Mikkola 2021](../cards/mikkola2021-aggressive-behaviour-is-affected-by-demographic-environmental-and.md)
