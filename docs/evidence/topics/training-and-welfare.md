# Behavior modification and training welfare

## What is supported
OpenPaw follows the AVSAB reward-based position; observational work found more coded welfare-risk behaviors and larger cortisol changes in dogs attending highly aversive schools than in reward-based schools. [AVSAB 2021](../cards/avsab2021-position-statement-on-humane-dog-training.md) [Vieira de Castro 2020](../cards/vieiradecastro2020-does-training-method-matter-evidence-for-the-negative.md)

A kennel pilot reported less barking after counterconditioning, and a small shelter comparison found short-term reductions in dog-directed aggression after rehabilitation. [Zurlinden 2022](../cards/zurlinden2022-impact-of-classical-counterconditioning-quiet-kennel-exercise-on.md) [Orihel & Fraser 2008](../cards/orihel2008-a-note-on-the-effectiveness-of-behavioural-rehabilitation.md)

## What is contested
A controlled field comparison also found more tension and yawning during electronic-collar training than during reward-based training, without a clear owner-reported efficacy advantage. [Cooper 2014](../cards/cooper2014-the-welfare-consequences-and-efficacy-of-training-pet.md)

An electronic-collar comparison found short-term suppression in a lure task, but transfer to a novel arena was poor and the comparator and welfare measures have been challenged; this is not a training recommendation. [Johnson 2024](../cards/johnson2024-comparison-of-the-efficacy-and-welfare-of-different.md) [Bangura 2025](../cards/bangura2025-a-critical-evaluation-of-johnson-and-wynnes-2024.md) [AVSAB 2021](../cards/avsab2021-position-statement-on-humane-dog-training.md)

## What is unknown
This sweep did not identify a comparative outcome study that isolated branded Behavior Adjustment Training (BAT) or Look at That (LAT) for leash reactivity, so lessons should distinguish documented learning principles from claims about named protocols. [Shnookal 2025](../cards/shnookal2025-exploring-trainer-insights-and-understanding-of-on-lead.md) [Orihel & Fraser 2008](../cards/orihel2008-a-note-on-the-effectiveness-of-behavioural-rehabilitation.md) [Riemer 2023](../cards/riemer2023-therapy-and-prevention-of-noise-fears-in-dogs.md)
