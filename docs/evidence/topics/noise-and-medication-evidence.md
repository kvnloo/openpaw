# Noise responses and medication evidence

## What is supported
Owners report responses to routine household sounds as well as fireworks, and blinded trials for specific fireworks or storm contexts reported reductions in caregiver-rated signs versus placebo. [Grigg 2021](../cards/grigg2021-stress-related-behaviors-in-companion-dogs-exposed-to.md) [Engel 2019](../cards/engel2019-effectiveness-of-imepitoin-for-the-control-of-anxiety.md) [Korpivaara 2017](../cards/korpivaara2017-dexmedetomidine-oromucosal-gel-for-noise-associated-acute-anxiety.md) [Perdew 2021](../cards/perdew2021-evaluation-of-pexion-imepitoin-for-treatment-of-storm.md)

OpenPaw can record sound class, event timing, observed response and whether the outcome is caregiver-rated or device-measured; medication data belong in historical notes, not recommendations. [Grigg 2021](../cards/grigg2021-stress-related-behaviors-in-companion-dogs-exposed-to.md) [Weng 2025](../cards/weng2025-real-world-data-on-behavioral-practices-for-dogs.md) [Korpivaara 2017](../cards/korpivaara2017-dexmedetomidine-oromucosal-gel-for-noise-associated-acute-anxiety.md)

## What is contested
Caregivers reported perceived improvement with several approaches, but uncontrolled surveys cannot distinguish treatment effects, differing exposures and reporting effects; mixed-intervention case series cannot isolate drug effects. [Riemer 2020](../cards/riemer2020-effectiveness-of-treatments-for-firework-fears-in-dogs.md) [Gruen 2020](../cards/gruen2020-evaluation-of-repeated-dosing-of-a-dexmedetomidine-oromucosal.md) [McPeake 2017](../cards/mcpeake2017-the-use-of-imepitoin-pexiontm-on-fear-and.md)

## What is unknown
Event-specific placebo comparisons cannot determine effectiveness in a different exposure, and these sources do not establish a general ranking of medications for canine behavioral presentations. [Engel 2019](../cards/engel2019-effectiveness-of-imepitoin-for-the-control-of-anxiety.md) [Perdew 2021](../cards/perdew2021-evaluation-of-pexion-imepitoin-for-treatment-of-storm.md) [Williamson 2024](../cards/williamson2024-comparing-the-effectiveness-of-clomipramine-and-fluoxetine-in.md) [Erickson 2021](../cards/erickson2021-a-review-of-pre-appointment-medications-to-reduce.md)
