# Owner reports, scales and aging

## What is supported
In the Dog Aging Project, short and long C-BARQ forms agreed better at the domain level than at the individual-item level, and owner-reported disease history agreed with clinical records to differing degrees by disease category. [Wilkins 2024](../cards/wilkins2024-validation-of-the-shortened-version-of-the-canine.md) [Schmid 2026](../cards/schmid2026-accuracy-of-owner-reported-diagnoses-for-dogs-enrolled.md)

OpenPaw should preserve form version, reporting period, rater and missing items; longitudinal trends in one dog's records are distinct from comparisons between cohorts of different ages. [Pellowe 2025](../cards/pellowe2025-dog-guardian-interpretation-of-familiar-dog-aggression-questions.md) [Wilkins 2024](../cards/wilkins2024-validation-of-the-shortened-version-of-the-canine.md) [Li 2025](../cards/li2025-an-analysis-of-behavioral-characteristics-and-enrollment-year.md) [Lee 2022](../cards/lee2022-age-and-physical-activity-levels-in-companion-dogs.md)

## What is contested
Lower activity was associated with older age and cognitive classification at baseline, but cross-sectional associations cannot establish whether activity changes precede cognitive changes, follow them or reflect additional factors. [Lee 2022](../cards/lee2022-age-and-physical-activity-levels-in-companion-dogs.md) [Yarborough 2022](../cards/yarborough2022-evaluation-of-cognitive-function-in-the-dog-aging.md) [Hargrave 2026](../cards/hargrave2026-functional-assessments-of-short-term-spatial-memory-in.md)

## What is unknown
The search did not establish what instrument “LOTE” denotes or identify a validated basis for converting different owner scales into a single score; even translated versions have context-specific psychometric support. [Wilkins 2024](../cards/wilkins2024-validation-of-the-shortened-version-of-the-canine.md) [Mills 2020](../cards/mills2020-development-and-psychometric-validation-of-the-lincoln-canine.md) [de Souza 2025](../cards/desouza2025-adaptation-and-psychometric-properties-of-the-brazilian-version.md) [Mackay 2023](../cards/mackay2023-trait-level-resilience-in-pet-dogs-development-of.md)
