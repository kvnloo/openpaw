# Pain, mobility and behavior

## What is supported
Clinical evidence links pain or itch with behavior differences, and dogs with the same orthopedic condition can show very different clinical and quality-of-life scores. [Lopes Fagundes 2018](../cards/lopesfagundes2018-noise-sensitivities-in-dogs-an-exploration-of-signs.md) [McAuliffe 2022](../cards/mcauliffe2022-associations-between-atopic-dermatitis-and-anxiety-aggression-and.md) [Dorlis 2026](../cards/dorlis2026-prospective-comparison-of-chronic-pain-and-quality-of.md)

OpenPaw's vet view can show when changes in movement, sleep or tolerance were first noticed alongside caregiver notes and measured activity, without classifying an animal as being in pain. [Medina-Serra 2025](../cards/medinaserra2025-initial-validation-of-the-lspain-scale-a-behaviour.md) [Malkani 2026](../cards/malkani2026-assessment-of-the-relationship-between-sleep-and-welfare.md) [Stevens 2025](../cards/stevens2025-factors-influencing-and-associated-with-physical-activity-patterns.md)

## What is contested
Activity can change with some clinical interventions, but a separate small placebo comparison found no clear activity or pain-score difference; a monitor is not a direct pain measure. [Brown 2010](../cards/brown2010-use-of-an-activity-monitor-to-detect-response.md) [Pedersen 2026](../cards/pedersen2026-effect-of-transcutaneous-electrical-nerve-stimulation-on-physical.md) [Stevens 2025](../cards/stevens2025-factors-influencing-and-associated-with-physical-activity-patterns.md)

## What is unknown
The case series does not establish whether later-onset noise sensitivity identifies pain or how often that pattern occurs outside referrals. [Lopes Fagundes 2018](../cards/lopesfagundes2018-noise-sensitivities-in-dogs-an-exploration-of-signs.md)
