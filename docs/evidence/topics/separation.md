# Separation-related behavior

## What is supported
Separation-related signs varied across dogs, and caregiver-reported changes in time alone were associated with new reports during pandemic schedule shifts. [Lenkei 2018](../cards/lenkei2018-fear-vs-frustration-possible-factors-behind-canine-separation.md) [de Assis 2019](../cards/deassis2019-developing-diagnostic-frameworks-in-veterinary-behavioral-medicine-disambiguating.md) [Harvey 2022](../cards/harvey2022-impact-of-changes-in-time-left-alone-on.md)

OpenPaw can retain the timing and source of observations, distinguishing video-observed vocalizing or pacing from signs noticed only on return. [Lenkei 2018](../cards/lenkei2018-fear-vs-frustration-possible-factors-behind-canine-separation.md) [Sargisson 2014](../cards/sargisson2014-canine-separation-anxiety-strategies-for-treatment-and-management.md) [Harvey 2022](../cards/harvey2022-impact-of-changes-in-time-left-alone-on.md)

One small within-dog comparison reported fewer or less severe behaviors during combined behavioral modification; it cannot establish which component helped. [Butler 2011](../cards/butler2011-the-efficacy-of-systematic-desensitization-for-treating-the.md)

## What is contested
One brief separation test found no clear salivary copeptin difference between groups; a separate small vasopressin experiment is preliminary and does not validate autonomic or saliva proxies as detectors. [Pierantoni 2022](../cards/pierantoni2022-signs-of-anxiety-and-salivary-copeptin-levels-in.md) [Pirrone 2019](../cards/pirrone2019-salivary-vasopressin-as-a-potential-non-invasive-biomarker.md)

## What is unknown
There is no supported single latency or tolerance cutoff in this set, and survey groupings do not establish a particular dog's motivation or exclude physical contributors. [de Assis 2019](../cards/deassis2019-developing-diagnostic-frameworks-in-veterinary-behavioral-medicine-disambiguating.md) [Hauser 2024](../cards/hauser2024-separation-related-problems-and-their-interaction-with-physical.md) [Lenkei 2018](../cards/lenkei2018-fear-vs-frustration-possible-factors-behind-canine-separation.md)
