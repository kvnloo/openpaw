# Sleep, activity and enrichment

## What is supported
Canine activity patterns varied between owned free-roaming, farm and family dogs; polysomnography in a different study also showed that recording context affected sleep staging. [Griss 2021](../cards/griss2021-if-they-could-choose-how-would-dogs-spend.md) [Baranyai 2025](../cards/baranyai2025-family-dogs-sleep-macrostructure-reflects-worsened-sleep-quality.md)

OpenPaw can compare activity and quiet intervals within a dog by time of day and routine, while keeping caregiver-reported sleep, inactivity and EEG-defined sleep distinct. [Griss 2021](../cards/griss2021-if-they-could-choose-how-would-dogs-spend.md) [Mondino 2023](../cards/mondino2023-development-and-validation-of-a-sleep-questionnaire-snore.md) [Straube-Koegler 2025](../cards/straubekoegler2025-actical-accelerometers-as-a-clinical-tool-for-the.md)

## What is contested
Social activity and exercise correlate with some reported behavior outcomes, but those associations do not establish that increasing exercise changes fear; short enrichment studies report activity-specific results. [Hakanen 2020](../cards/hakanen2020-active-and-social-life-is-associated-with-lower.md) [Tiira 2015](../cards/tiira2015-early-life-experiences-and-exercise-associate-with-canine.md) [Hunt 2022](../cards/hunt2022-effects-of-environmental-enrichment-on-dog-behaviour-pilot.md)

## What is unknown
These samples do not provide a validated universal daily sleep-hour target from collar motion or a fixed activity budget for a pet, puppy or senior dog. [Straube-Koegler 2025](../cards/straubekoegler2025-actical-accelerometers-as-a-clinical-tool-for-the.md) [Dale 2024](../cards/dale2024-canine-separation-related-behaviour-at-six-months-of.md) [Griss 2021](../cards/griss2021-if-they-could-choose-how-would-dogs-spend.md) [Lee 2022](../cards/lee2022-age-and-physical-activity-levels-in-companion-dogs.md)
